# Google Play — Notas de la versión 4.0.3

## English (What's new)

PocketTool 4.0.3
• New People section: manage shared expenses and see each person's payment history.
• Shared expenses in fixed and financings, with your share, total and shared views in summaries, budgets and credits.
• Pending commitments in Records and today's obligations on Home.
• Vehicle: better odometer inference, remembered fuel units and clearer linked-expense checks.
• Fixed amounts, modal scrolling and swipe gestures.

## Español (Novedades)

PocketTool 4.0.3

• Gastos compartidos en fijos y financiamientos, con vistas de tu parte, total y compartido en resúmenes, presupuestos y créditos.
